# Code of conduct

UniteDesk welcomes people who want to improve team workflows. Be considerate and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews, and project discussions.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and code, not people. Explain the reason for a requested change.
- Assume good intent, ask clarifying questions, and make space for newcomers.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Protect other people's private data. Use synthetic examples in public reports.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and repeated hostile or disruptive behavior are not accepted. Spam and attempts to pressure people into starring, forking, or endorsing the project are also unwelcome.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a project conversation that needs moderation, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/nokibuzz/unitedesk/issues/new/choose) with links to the relevant public comments. Do not include private details in that issue. For threats, harassment, or other sensitive abuse on GitHub, use [GitHub's private content report](https://github.com/contact/report-content). For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock threads, and limit participation. Reports will be assessed based on the behavior and context, without promising a fixed response time.
